Our Journey

The Story So Far

1924

The Birth of a Dream

Jean-Paul Aurore, a young chemist from Lyon with an extraordinary sense of smell, opens a small perfumery at 12 Rue du Faubourg Saint-Honoré, Paris. With a single fragrance — Aurore Naissante — he captures the attention of Parisian society.

1936

Royal Recognition

Maison Aurore is granted the Royal Warrant of the Belgian Court. Queen Elisabeth personally selects Iris de Minuit as her signature fragrance, catapulting the house to international fame. The waiting list for custom creations extends to two years.

1952

The Grasse Connection

Jean-Paul establishes a dedicated extraction facility in Grasse, the world capital of perfume. Direct partnerships with rose, jasmine, and tuberose farmers allow Maison Aurore to secure the finest botanical harvests for generations to come.

1968

The Second Generation

Marie-Claude Aurore, Jean-Paul's daughter, becomes Chief Perfumer. Her radical vision — blending Eastern oud traditions with French floral techniques — produces Nuit de Bagdad, which becomes the house's best-selling fragrance of the 20th century.

1985

Global Expansion

Maison Aurore opens its first boutiques outside France — London, New York, Tokyo, and Dubai. Each location is designed as an intimate salon rather than a store, offering private consultations and bespoke fragrance services.

2001

The Sustainability Pledge

Under the leadership of Henri Aurore (3rd generation), Maison Aurore becomes the first luxury perfume house to publish a full ingredient transparency report and commits to 100% ethically sourced botanical ingredients by 2010 — a goal achieved three years ahead of schedule.

2015

The Digital Atelier

Étienne Aurore (4th generation) launches the Maison's first digital presence, pioneering the concept of virtual fragrance consultations. The Scent Profile tool, powered by olfactive science, is used by over 2 million customers worldwide.

2024

A Century, and Beyond

Maison Aurore celebrates its centenary with Le Grand Siècle — a limited collection of 100 masterwork fragrances, one for each year of the Maison's history. Each bottle is hand-numbered, presented in a custom mahogany case, and priced from $2,400.

The Aurore Legacy

Four Generations,

What began as one man's dream in a small Paris workshop has grown into one of the world's most respected perfume houses. Yet the spirit remains unchanged: an absolute devotion to the art of fragrance, and a belief that the finest things in life are invisible.
