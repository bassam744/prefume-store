Our Philosophy

Fragrance as a
Second Skin

We believe a perfume is not merely a scent — it is memory, emotion, and identity rendered invisible. Since 1924, Maison Aurore has worked with the world's rarest raw materials to create fragrances that become inseparable from those who wear them.

Every bottle is hand-filled and inspected by our master perfumers in Grasse, France — the perfume capital of the world.

From Field to Flacon

The Aurore Process

01

Source

We partner with small-batch growers in Grasse, Bulgaria, and Madagascar to select only the most exceptional botanical harvests.

02

Compose

Our master perfumers weave together top, heart, and base notes with the precision of a composer writing a symphony.

03

Macerate

Each fragrance macerates for a minimum of six weeks, allowing the ingredients to meld into a seamless, complex accord.

04

Bottle

Hand-poured into our iconic crystal flacons, each bottle is individually numbered and sealed with our signature wax emblem.
