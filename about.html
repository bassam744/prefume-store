Who We Are

A House Built on
Rareness

Maison Aurore was founded in Paris in 1924 by master perfumer Jean-Paul Aurore, with a singular conviction: that perfume is the most intimate of all art forms — invisible, yet unforgettable.

A century later, that conviction guides every decision we make. We source only the finest raw materials from the most pristine corners of the world. We work with artisans who share our uncompromising standards. We never rush the creative process.

The result is a collection of fragrances that transcend trends, seasons, and time — fragrances that become part of who you are.

Our Pillars

What We Stand For

Rarity

We seek the exceptional: rose absolute from Grasse, aged oud from Laos, iris butter from Tuscany. Only the rarest ingredients meet our standard.

Patience

Great perfume cannot be hurried. Our fragrances macerate for months. Our perfumers spend years refining a single accord. We never compromise for speed.

Excellence

Every bottle is a promise. Our quality control is absolute — each fragrance must pass the approval of our chief perfumer before it reaches your hands.

Sustainability

We are committed to ethical sourcing, responsible harvesting, and ensuring that the world's rarest botanical treasures endure for generations to come.

Mission

To Create
the Invisible

Our mission is to craft fragrances that transcend the ordinary — scents that carry memory, evoke emotion, and define identity. We exist to bring the art of perfumery to those who understand that luxury is felt, not seen.

Vision

A World Where Every
Moment has a Scent

We envision a future where fine perfumery is accessible to those who truly value it — where the ritual of choosing and wearing a great fragrance is understood as one of life's profound pleasures.
